# Privacy checklist

This reference lists privacy and compliance items to verify during a review. It is a verification aid, not legal advice.

## Data collection and documentation

- Data collected, purposes, retention periods and recipients are documented.
- Privacy policy and required notices are accessible.

## Legal basis and rights

- A legal basis for processing is defined where applicable (for example consent or contract).
- User rights (access, rectification, deletion, objection) and how to exercise them are documented.

## Consent and cookies

- Consent is collected where required.
- Non-essential cookies are not set before consent.
- Refusal is as easy as acceptance where applicable.

## Payments and contracts

- Payment flows are tested in sandbox or equivalent environments where applicable.
- Terms such as CGU, CGV or refund policies are in place when relevant.

## Local obligations

- Local legal requirements are checked with an official source or a competent professional.
- Adaptations for non-EU projects are noted explicitly when relevant.

## References

- CNIL: https://www.cnil.fr/
- RGPD: https://gdpr.eu/ (and official EU sources)

## Notes

- Privacy obligations depend on country, activity and data processed.
- This reference helps identify what to check; it does not certify compliance.
